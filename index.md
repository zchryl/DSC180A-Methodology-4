1. **What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**  
The most interesting topic covered so far for Quarter 1 is Fractal Chain of Thought, which is a prompting approach that deploys recursion to the prompting of modern LLMs. Instead of giving straightforward, sometimes ambiguous prompts to AI, Fractal Chain of Thought (FCoT) explicitly asks the LLM to look at 3 different levels of information: the macro, the meso, and the micro. All of these layers focus the scope of the LLM onto an increasingly tighter population or source. For example, the macro scale of information could be the internet. The meso scale of information could be news headlines or state-specific postings. At the micro level could be individual user posts on a topic. At each layer of FCoT, objective functions are given to the LLM: minimize hype or sensationalist sources, maximize veracity by finding articles on the same topic, etc. This is the most interesting topic to me because it demonstrates the practice of intelligent AI usage. Instead of blindly sending in prompts and taking the outputs at face value, believing they are 100% correct, FCoT adds more user instructions and requires more tokens to get the LLM thinking about fact checking itself. FCoT represents a practice where hallucinations are mitigated and sycophancy is ignored, because there are so much recursive steps being taken in this one prompt unlike a regular, straightforward question.
